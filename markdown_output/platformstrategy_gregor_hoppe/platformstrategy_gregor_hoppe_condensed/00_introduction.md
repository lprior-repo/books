# **About this Book**

This book emerged from years of building platforms across financial services, government (Singapore GovTech), cloud providers, and serverless products. Each experience raised more questions about platform strategy, design trade-offs, and implementation decisions.

![](_page_1_Picture_5.jpeg)

## **The Lure of Platforms**

Over the past decade, platforms have fueled unprecedented success—7 of the 10 most valuable companies in 2018 used platform business models (zero in 2008). Platforms share an "amplifier effect" through:

- **Marketplace flywheel**: More sellers → wider selection → more buyers → more sellers (eBay, Uber, Airbnb)
- **IT multiplier**: Shared foundations enable diverse solutions, hiding complexity to accelerate value delivery
- **Automotive heritage**: Amortizing engineering investments across models with different styling

## **Building IT Platforms**

Despite apparent benefits, many platform initiatives fail—consuming years of investment only to launch obsolete or unused. This highlights the need for clear strategy and deep understanding of platform constraints and assumptions.

### **What Will I Learn?**

This book condenses platform experience into a mental framework covering technical, organizational, and financial aspects:

**Part I: Understanding Platforms** - Platform history, types, and benefits
**Part II: A Strategy for Platforms** - Investment, strategy, and actionable decisions
**Part III: In-House Platforms** - Characteristics of internal platform initiatives
**Part IV: Designing Platforms** - Design decisions illustrated through metaphors
**Part V: Implementing Platforms** - Platform anatomies and common blueprints
**Part VI: Growing Platforms** - Rollout, evolution, and avoiding entropy/bottlenecks
**Part VII: Organizing for Platforms** - Building and managing platform teams
